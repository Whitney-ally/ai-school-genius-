📚 Study Tips

Small habits can make a big difference.

⏱️ Try focused study sessions
Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break.
🧠 Practise active recall
Close your notes and explain the idea from memory before checking your notes.
🔁 Review more than once
Revisit difficult topics the next day and again a few days later.
✍️ Learn from mistakes
Write down what confused you and practise a similar question.
😴 Protect your sleep
A regular sleep routine helps you stay focused and ready to learn.
